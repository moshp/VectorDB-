SARS Guide Topic Map

Each point is one ~200-token passage from a SARS guide, placed by the meaning of its text. Passages about similar subjects sit close together. Colour and shape show the topic it was grouped into; hover a point to read it, or pick a topic on the right to isolate it.

    Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. t-SNE keeps near neighbours together; distances between far-apart groups don’t mean much.
